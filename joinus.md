  <div class="container prose">
    <h2 class="title">✅ STEP 1: The smell test — fit is all you need</h2>

    <p>Interested in joining our lab? Thank you very much for your interest!</p>

    <p>You will spend several years with us, so it is important to make sure that our lab is a good fit for you. Fit depends on many things: your research interests, the lab environment, your expectations, your working style, and so on. We strongly recommend that you explore our lab with all five senses before applying; otherwise, we may both end up unhappy!</p>

    <p>Here are some ways to do this:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>Go through every page of <a href="/">our website</a>. Who works here, and how many students are there? How often do we publish, in which areas and at which venues? What expertise do we have? Is the lab well funded? Do we enjoy our research? What does a typical day look like?</li>
      <li>Read <a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/english/laboratory/aiw/inoue.html">the lab intro webpage at JAIST</a> (日本語版は<a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/laboratory/aiw/inoue.html">こちら</a>), where Prof. Inoue describes the basic skills he expects from students, how he supervises, and the lab's research direction.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="alt" id="step-2">
  <div class="container prose">
    <h2 class="title">✅ STEP 2: How to join</h2>

    <h3 class="subtitle">For JAIST students</h3>

    <div class="callout">
      <span class="ico">!</span>
      <div><strong>WARNING:</strong> We only accept students who have contacted Prof. Inoue before enrolling at JAIST. This policy ensures that (a) there is a good match between students and the lab and that (b) Prof. Inoue can dedicate sufficient time to supervising each student.</div>
    </div>

    <h3 class="subtitle">For non-JAIST students</h3>

    <p>Our lab belongs to <a href="http://www.jaist.ac.jp/english/">JAIST</a>, a graduate university in Ishikawa, Japan. JAIST offers both master's and doctoral programs, and welcomes students from around the world at its Ishikawa campus and working professionals (社会人学生) at its Tokyo satellite campus.</p>

    <div class="callout">
      <span class="ico">i</span>
      <div><strong>NOTE:</strong> Students admitted to the Ishikawa campus through the regular examinations are not guaranteed a place in our lab. To secure a place in advance, use one of the following schemes:
        <ul>
          <li>Informal pre-approval system (<a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/education/procedures/lab-assignment.html">配属研究室入学前内定制度</a>)</li>
          <li>For international students only: Examination for Admission on Recommendation for Overseas Residents (<a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/english/admissions/application-guide/guide-m.html">Master's</a> or <a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/english/admissions/application-guide/guide-d.html">Doctoral</a>)</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="callout">
      <span class="ico">!</span>
      <div><strong>WARNING:</strong> Due to limited capacity, we are currently not accepting master's students in the working-professional program (社会人修士課程) at the Tokyo satellite campus. If you are interested in the doctoral program for working professionals (社会人博士課程), please contact us.</div>
    </div>

    <p>For details, please read <a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/english/admissions/">the admissions information</a> (日本語版は<a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/admissions/">こちら</a>) and find the scheme that suits you.</p>

    <p>For financial support, see <a href="https://www.jaist.ac.jp/english/studentlife/support/scholarships.html">JAIST's guide to scholarships</a>.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="step-3">
  <div class="container prose">
    <h2 class="title">✅ STEP 3: Talk to us</h2>

    <p>Please schedule a lab tour (in person or online). The tour usually goes as follows:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>An overview of the lab: research projects and lab activities (10 min)</li>
      <li>Free discussion: why you are interested in our lab, your background, your research plan, and any questions you have (20 min)</li>
    </ul>

    <div class="callout">
      <span class="ico">★</span>
      <div><strong>IMPORTANT:</strong> Before the tour, please think about your research plan and what you want to ask. The conversation is your chance to find out whether our lab is a good fit for you.</div>
    </div>

    <p>How to make an appointment:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>Check available times in the calendar below.</li>
      <li>Please <a href="/contact.html">contact Prof. Inoue via email</a> (日本語可), indicating:
        <ul>
          <li>You want to schedule a lab tour.</li>
          <li>How you want to join us (see STEP 2).</li>
          <li>Your preferred dates and times for the tour (a 30-minute slot).</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
    </ul>

    <p>We look forward to speaking with you!</p>
