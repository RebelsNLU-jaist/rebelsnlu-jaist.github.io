Who we are

An international lab for AI & NLP

Run by Associate Professor Naoya Inoue at JAIST since 2022.

RebelsNLU is an international research lab for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP), run by Associate Professor Naoya Inoue at Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST). RebelsNLU is short for Reading between the Lines for Natural Language Understanding.

One important mission of a university research lab is to develop students’ skills. We design lab activities so that students acquire general-purpose skills for problem-finding and problem-solving, on top of expertise in natural language processing research and related areas.

Students learn how to think critically, how to plan, how to present their work, how to dive into unknown fields, how to program, and how to work as a team. Our motto: research is the best on-the-job training for developing such skills. To grow globally successful students, we use English as our common working language.
